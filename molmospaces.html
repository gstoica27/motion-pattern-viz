MolmoSpaces — Motion Magnitudes

20 random MolmoSpaces samples (sim ground-truth tracks). Each chart shows the per-frame average 3D delta magnitude (top-20% trimmed) per body, with kept clip ranges highlighted. Threshold = 0.001 (sim motion is much quieter than tracked data; 0.02 captures almost nothing on molmo and 0.01 barely any, so this subpage uses a looser threshold to surface the bulk of the activity).
